## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

- **Q: What is a WMM Plant used for?**  
  **A:** A Wet Mix Macadam Plant is used to produce a properly mixed combination of aggregates and water for road base and sub-base construction.

- **Q: What should contractors consider when selecting a WMM Plant?**  
  **A:** Contractors should evaluate project requirements, production needs, plant configuration, aggregate feeding arrangements, site conditions, and maintenance support.

- **Q: Why is a Four Bin Feeder important in a WMM Plant?**  
  **A:** A Four Bin Feeder helps store and feed different aggregate sizes separately, supporting controlled aggregate proportioning.

- **Q: How should a contractor evaluate a WMM Plant manufacturer?**  
  **A:** Review the manufacturer's product information, equipment configuration, technical support, installation assistance, and quotation details before making a decision.

- **Q: Can WMM Plant requirements vary between projects?**  
  **A:** Yes. Project specifications, production requirements, site conditions, and material requirements can influence the plant configuration.
